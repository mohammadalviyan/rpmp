# Grill me

Interview the user relentlessly about every aspect of the RPMP plan until both sides share the same understanding. Walk down each branch of the decision tree and resolve dependencies one by one. For each question, give a recommended answer.

Ask one question at a time.

If the answer exists in `docs/PRD.md`, an ADR, or the repo, inspect that source instead of asking. Cite stable FR, NFR, and persona IDs. Do not paste the full PRD into the conversation.
